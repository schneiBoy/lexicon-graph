LOVE [noun.]
ἀγάπη, love. [A word not found in the
profane writers, nor in Philo and
Josephus, nor in Acts, Mark, and
James. It is unknown to writers
outside of the N.T. φιλανθρωπία,
philanthropy was the highest word
used by the Greeks, which is a very
different thing to ἀγάπη, and even
far lower than φιλαδελφία. φιλαν-
θρωπία in its full display was only
giving to him who was entitled to it
his full rights.] ἀγάπη denotes
the love which springs from ad-
miration and veneration, and which
chooses its object with decision of
will, and devotes a self-denying and
compassionate devotion to it. Love
in its fullest conceivable form.
2. φιλανθρωπία, philanthropy, love of
man, (see above.)
